THE POPO - test build vVERSION

Double-click ThePopo-vVERSION.exe. If Windows warns about an unknown app,
choose More info, then Run anyway.

PLAYING
You start with the gun holstered: H, or holding right mouse, draws it.
Inside a building the floors above fade out, so nothing hangs over you.
The opening menu has Single Player (straight into the city), Online (host or
join friends, then the host presses Start the game), Settings and Quit. The
old test scenes are still there under Settings > Debug > Open a test scene. Esc in a game opens the menu: Resume,
Settings, Leave. Settings has Graphics (every option, with presets Low to
Max), Sound, Gameplay (friendly fire on/off, camera, keys) and Debug.

CONTENT AND POLISH (0.6.0)
New to the game? Pick FIRST SHIFT on the main menu: a step-by-step walk
through the job, from the station door to booking your first prisoner (it
never touches your saved city).
The radio on your shoulder now clicks, crackles and squelches with every
call, and other units chatter between jobs. There's music: a title theme,
a quiet bed on patrol, and it kicks in when the shooting starts or your
siren's on. Music and radio have their own volume sliders in Settings >
Sound.
More of the city: phone boxes, post boxes, vending machines, planters, bike
racks, wheelie bins and hydrants on the streets (different by district),
TVs, fridges, washing machines, armchairs, plants and bookcases indoors,
tyres and cones in the yards. On the roads: taxis, work vans with ladders,
couriers and the odd ice cream van. People wear a wider range of colours.

THE CITY LIVES (0.5.0)
Day and night: the light follows the clock. The sun comes up about six and
goes down about eight; at night the street lamps come on. The weather
changes now and then: clear, cloudy, rain, or a storm with thunder and
lightning. Rain makes the roads dark and shiny, and slippery: cars lose grip
until the roads dry out again.
Things just happen: car crashes (more in the rain), fires in buildings, and
burglar alarms going off. Get there, stay a while, and it's dealt with.
Repair crews: a while after something gets knocked down, a council crew
turns up (van at the kerb, hi-vis, cones). They cart the rubble away, road
first, then build it back up a piece at a time. Slow while you watch, quicker
when nobody's about. Scare them or hurt them and the work stops.
Saving: Continue now brings back the station's pay, everything bought at the
armoury, how far each story has got, the time of day, the weather, and the
kit each of you was carrying (by player name).
Host keys: K changes the weather, N moves the clock on three hours.

POLICE WORK (0.4.0)
Jobs now depend on where you are and the time of day (the clock is top left:
a day lasts 48 minutes). Muggings, shoplifters, fights, domestics, drug deals,
vandals and drunks out on the streets; burglaries, raids, hostage jobs and
building searches inside, floor by floor. Victims tell you what the suspect
looks like and which way they went. Some crimes just happen up the street
without a call: walk up and they're yours.
Y (d-pad up): shout "Police! Get down!". Unarmed suspects mostly give up on
the spot; drunks need to be close; armed ones only think about it.
Big busts: GANG WAR (two crews shooting it out: let them thin each other out,
then go in) and BANK JOB (armed robbers holding staff, a getaway driver out
front who drives off when the shooting starts).
Stories: three running stories (the Vipers gang, Councillor Hale, the Magpie)
come up between jobs. The same people come back, and if they got away last
time they're armed and they remember you.
PAY: every job closed and every suspect brought in pays the station (shown
next to the clock). Story characters brought in alive pay a bonus.
THE ARMOURY: walk onto the yellow ARMOURY circle just inside the station door
and press F. Spend the station's pay on six pistols, bench upgrades (bigger
mags, compensators, match barrels, speed loaders), hollow point and armour
piercing rounds, body armour, pouches, a garage, an interceptor, a prisoner
van, an unmarked car and a firing range. Each of you picks your own pistol,
rounds and loadout: ammo backpack, breach charges or C4 (press 2, aim and fire
to stick one on; 1 back to the pistol), or a tactical torch.
THE GUN BENCH: the armoury shows your pistol in 3D on a bench with every
upgrade the station owns fitted. Point at a pistol or an upgrade in the list
to see it there. Drag with the mouse to turn it, wheel to zoom; on a pad the
right stick turns it and the triggers zoom.
THE RANGE (once bought): out the back of the station. Stand on the blue
circle and press F for a 30 second run: shoot the red targets, not the blue.
UNDERCOVER (once plain clothes are bought, from the armoury): crooks take you
for anyone on the street until you draw your gun, shout, turn up in a marked
car or hang about too close (watch the suspicion meter). Stings: BAIT CAR
(let him take it; we shut it down) and SELLING (walk up in plain clothes, hold
F next to the dealer to buy, then take him).

THE WHOLE CITY (0.3.0)
The game is now a whole city: six by six blocks, about 370 m across, with
downtown offices and shops, houses and flats, an industrial district, two
parks, a car park and the docks on the water. You start at the police
station's front door; the patrol cars are parked out front and the custody
bay is round the side. The F3 overlay shows which district and street you are
on, and the radio names streets.
Every building can be entered (some doors are locked: kick them in), with
stairs on every floor and furnished rooms.
Traffic drives on the right and stops at the traffic lights. Put your siren
on and drivers pull over. Shoot near them and they floor it, or jump out
and run. People walk the pavements and scatter from gunfire. Cars hurt
anyone they hit, you included.
F at any car, van, lorry or motorbike gets you in (commandeering): the
driver is pulled out. Lorries pull trailers that swing out on corners and
can come off in a big crash.
New calls: CAR THEFT (someone breaking into a parked car; get there before
they drive off) and PURSUIT (a stolen car racing through town: ram it, box
it in or shoot it up, and the driver bails and runs).
Gunshots now crack, boom and roll round the streets; each district sounds
different, and sirens drop in pitch as they pass.
G (host) makes a new city, J (host) calls in a job now.

THE VIEW (v0.2.11: third person only)
The camera sits behind your officer's shoulder. The mouse looks around, the
dot in the middle of the screen is where shots go (hold right mouse to aim,
left mouse to fire), the mouse wheel moves the camera in and out, and it
works in cars too. Mouse speed: Settings > Gameplay. On a controller the
right stick looks around.

PLAYING OVER THE INTERNET
1. Choose Online, then press Host. The game asks your router to open port 27015 by itself and
   shows the address to give friends, with a button to copy it.
2. Friends type that address and port 27015 and press Join.
3. If Windows Firewall asks, allow The Popo on private and public networks.

If the launcher says your router did not open the port, forward it yourself:
  a. Find this PC's local address: open Command Prompt, type ipconfig, and
     note the IPv4 Address (like 192.168.1.23).
  b. Open your router's page in a browser (often http://192.168.1.1 or
     http://192.168.0.1; the address and password are usually on a sticker
     on the router).
  c. Find "Port forwarding" (sometimes under Advanced, NAT or Virtual servers).
  d. Add a rule: protocol UDP, external port 27015, internal port 27015,
     to this PC's local address. Save.
If the launcher warns about CGNAT or a second router, your internet provider
shares one address between customers; ask them for a public IP address.

KEYBOARD AND MOUSE (defaults)
WASD move, Shift sprint, Alt walk / run (toggle; a light tilt of the
controller stick walks too), Space jump / vault / climb (vault low walls, car
bonnets, and windows once the glass is smashed).
Ctrl: tap to crouch, tap again to stand; hold to lie prone; hold while
running to roll (slide when sprinting). Z prone and X roll also work.
C take cover at the nearest wall, car or ledge (back to it; low cover:
duck behind; at a wall's end the officer tucks in). Move away from it, or
press C, to leave. In cover, hold a direction and press C to run to the
next cover that way, or round the corner at the end of a wall. Hold right mouse to aim, and in cover to peek out (at a wall's end the
officer leans out round it);
fire without aiming in cover to blind fire.
Left mouse fire (at the reticle), R reload (15 rounds), H holster / draw,
T torch, middle mouse ping a spot for everyone.
V punch (while sprinting: shoulder barge), B kick (kicks doors in).
F use; hold F next to a downed teammate for 2 seconds to get them up.
G (hold) next to a downed teammate: grab their collar and drag them, walking
backwards, into cover. Let go of G to drop them.
Mouse looks round, mouse wheel moves the camera in and out, middle mouse pings a spot, F3 network overlay, Esc menu.
Ammo: bottom right shows rounds in the gun and spare rounds.
Health: shots (friendly fire, can be turned off in Settings), blasts and long falls hurt. At zero
you go down; a teammate gets you up, or you get up by yourself after 30 s.
POLICE CAR (1.3)
F at a door gets in (the nearest free seat); F again gets out. Ctrl moves
you to the next free seat. Up to three officers fit in one car.
Driver: WASD drive, Space handbrake, H horn, T lights, then lights and wail,
then yelp, then off. Passengers: hold right mouse to lean out of the window
and shoot. Windows shatter when shot; doors, bonnet, boot, bumpers and
mirrors come off in big crashes. Hold F at the boot for ammo and first aid.
HEALING YOUR FRIENDS (0.2.10)
Stand next to a hurt teammate and hold F: your first aid patches a wound
and gives back 45 health (25 when you use it on yourself). You carry 3;
refill at a police car's boot. Hurt teammates show their health above
their heads; a downed one says so (hold F on them to revive).
GOING DOWN, DRAGGING AND SOUND (0.2.11)
Bodies now fall the way they were going and lie stretched out instead of in
a heap. Hold G (Y on a controller) by a downed teammate to drag them by the
collar. Every gun, impact, glass, breakage, debris, explosion and gore sound
is now a real recording (Sonniss GDC game audio bundles).

GOING DOWN AND RESPAWNING (0.2.9)
When you go down, wait 3 seconds and press R to respawn where you started,
healed, with full ammo. Or a teammate can hold F on you to get you up
where you are, or after 30 seconds you get up by yourself.
FIGHTS AND MAYHEM (1.5)
Armed suspects now fight back from cover: they duck, peek, fire in bursts,
blind fire, go round your side and call friends. Shooting close past them
keeps their heads down. Unarmed ones throw chairs, crates and bottles.
New call: SHOTS FIRED, three or four armed, one with a shotgun.
When one gives up they toss their gun: F picks it up (you only get the
rounds in it), H drops it.
Furniture and junk break: chairs, tables, shelves, crates, bottles. Red
barrels and gas bottles explode and start fires. Fire spreads to wooden
things and burns. Shoot a red extinguisher to put out fires round it.
Cars smoke when badly shot up, then catch fire and blow up a few seconds
later (shots low at the back hit the fuel tank).
Camera shake strength: Settings > Gameplay > Camera shake.
SOUND (1.6)
Turn your speakers or headphones up: gunshots, bullet hits and near misses,
footsteps, engines, the street outside and breaking glass all play now.
Settings > Sound has sliders for each part. To hear everything on its own,
open Settings > Debug > Open a test scene > 1.6 Listening room: 5, 6 and 7
fire test guns in the street, the shop and behind a wall, 8 fires a shotgun
next to you, 9 sends bullets past your head, 0 sets off a blast, F4 fires
the test guns in turn. Shoot the shop's glass, door and walls, the car and
the crates.
CALLS AND ARRESTS (1.4)
Calls come in over the radio (top of the screen) and are listed top left
with their distance; arrows at the screen edge point the way.
Suspects run and hide, and armed ones may shoot. Aim at one up close, or
punch them, and they give up and kneel. Hold F next to them to cuff them;
they follow you. F at a police car door puts them in the back. Drive to
the CUSTODY bay (yellow box, blue lamp) to book them.
Speeding car: get behind it with the lights on (T) and it pulls over, then
walk up to the driver. J (host) calls in a job straight away.
INJURIES
Shots can wound: a leg wound makes you limp (no sprint), an arm wound shakes
your aim, bleeding drains health. Hold F to treat yourself or a teammate
(uses one first aid from your pouch of 3; one wound per use). The car boot
fixes everything and refills the pouch.
Civilian box car (test scenes): WASD drive, Space handbrake, H horn.
In the city: 1 pistol, 2 demolition charge.
Training yard (1.2): everything above in one place; hold F at the ammo box
for spare rounds. K (host) puts you down
to try reviving, G (host) resets the yard.
Look scene: N day / night.
